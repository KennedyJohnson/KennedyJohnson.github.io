Projects

Gopher X Metro

One live map of every U of M campus bus alongside Metro Transit buses and light rail, rebuilt to run free with no API keys. I built it in 2024 with a team from Social Coding at UMN and revived it in 2026 after the original hosting lapsed.

Delay Forecast: Will My Bus Be Late?

Pick a route, stop and time to see how often each departure runs late or early. LightGBM models retrained daily on live-feed history and weather run right in the browser.

For each scheduled Metro Transit departure you see how late it usually runs, the chance it is 5+ minutes late, and the chance it leaves early, so you can pick which bus to catch.

Twin Cities Living Quality Map

Scores every Minneapolis and St. Paul district from 0 to 100 on safety, opportunity, amenities, transportation and economic profile, using public data refreshed automatically. Search any address to see its breakdown and multi-year trends.

Minneapolis Home Assessment Check

Checks Minneapolis home assessments against real sales, with a price model more accurate than the city's (9.4% vs 12.0% error). It never sees the city's values, and each home links back to the map for its neighborhood.

Type a Minneapolis address to compare the assessed value with time-adjusted prices of similar nearby sales, plus an independent estimate of this month's sale price with a likely range.

Will My Flight Be Delayed?

Enter a US flight number and date to get the chance it arrives 15+ minutes late, with a route map and live weather. A LightGBM model trained on 7M flights picks out delayed flights 73% of the time, versus 62% from a flight's past on-time record alone.

Movie Recommender

Rate a few movies and get a ranked list of what to watch next. The model behind it won my benchmark of eight recommenders on 33.8M ratings, ranking relevant movies about 7x better than a standard baseline.

My Social Media, as Data 2026

Fourteen years of my own Instagram, Reddit, YouTube and other exports, published only as aggregates. It shows how recommendation algorithms took over my feeds, with my top 10 accounts going from most of my likes to under 10%.
